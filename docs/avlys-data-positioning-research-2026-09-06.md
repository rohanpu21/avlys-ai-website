# Avlys Data: competitor research and recommended launch position

Research date: 6 September 2026. Status: research and recommendations only; no website implementation or sample publication.

## Recommendation in brief

Introduce **Avlys Data** as a proposed division of Avlys, with its own `/data` page and buyer journey. This is a branding recommendation, not a statement that a separate company has been incorporated.

Lead with **text, image, and voice data for AI training and evaluation**. These match the sample strengths you described. Keep **egocentric capture** visible as a smaller, clearly scoped pilot offering, not the headline promise.

The initial objective should be to help a qualified buyer evaluate a relevant collection and commission a paid pilot. Do not start by building an open marketplace or advertising every imaginable data service.

Your sample ownership, commercial licensing rights, public-display permissions, quantities, languages, domains, annotation quality, and collection capacity have not yet been verified. Recommendations below are a proposed direction, not claims ready to publish unchanged.

## 1. Scope and method

Reviewed eight complementary providers through their official websites: Shaip, Appen, Defined.ai, iMerit, Innodata, Claru, MoVo, and Scale. Inspected homepages and relevant collection, catalog, dataset, or robotics pages. Also examined Defined.ai and Shaip catalog layouts in a desktop browser. This was not an end-to-end website QA exercise.

The competitor-profiling skill supplied a consistent comparison framework: positioning, buyer, deliverable, proof, commercial path, and implication for Avlys. Its preferred scraping/SEO tools were unavailable, so this review used web/browser inspection and saved bounded source notes instead. No traffic, backlink, market-size, customer-review, or comparable price dataset was obtained. Vendor claims about quantities, certifications, customers, and performance were not independently audited. An Innodata product page could not be reliably re-extracted; detailed claims from it were excluded.

The earlier research note in this project remains unchanged. This report incorporates your clarification that text, image, and voice are stronger than egocentric samples.

## 2. What the companies sell, and what to learn

| Provider | Observed positioning/presentation | Recommendation for Avlys |
|---|---|---|
| [Shaip](https://www.shaip.com/) | Broad collection, annotation, licensing, and evaluation offer | Separate existing datasets from custom production and labeling services. |
| [Appen](https://www.appen.com/physical-ai) | Model-use-case organization with dataset formats and quantity units | State the task each collection supports and what its quantity measures. |
| [Defined.ai](https://defined.ai/datasets) | Searchable catalog organized by type, language, domain, locale, and amount | Make technical sample discovery the center of the page. |
| [iMerit](https://imerit.ai/domains/robotics/video-data-collection/) | Capture protocols, task taxonomy, and review process | Demonstrate your actual collection and QA process. |
| [Innodata](https://innodata.com/) | Training/evaluation task categories alongside enterprise AI | Keep software development and data procurement journeys distinct. |
| [Claru](https://claru.ai/datasets/egocentric-kitchen-video) | Narrow task-specific dataset pages with capture and label details | Show exactly what an egocentric pilot contains. |
| [MoVo](https://www.movodata.com/) | Preparation tiers and a pilot-led sale | Explain deliverable depth and acceptance criteria. |
| [Scale](https://scale.com/physical-ai) | Collection engineering, hardware, annotation, and validation | Do not claim robotics infrastructure merely because video samples exist. |

These are useful references, not a ranked list of the best suppliers. Most inspected sales paths lead to a conversation or quote; the review did not establish comparable numeric prices.

### A. Availability needs to be explicit

Shaip distinguishes sample-ready from partner-led collections. Its physical-AI catalog also discloses that displayed quantities are illustrative ranges and imagery is representative stock. This is a useful distinction between advertised capability and inspectable inventory. [Catalog source](https://www.shaip.com/offerings/physical-ai-data-catalog/).

For Avlys, label each entry separately: **preview approved**, **licensing available**, **custom collection**, or **pilot**. A sample does not establish a production-scale collection. Use actual cleared media in portfolio cards; do not present stock or generated examples as collected training data.

### B. Technical details build a useful portfolio

Defined.ai is the strongest catalog reference for this particular page because it helps buyers narrow a collection before talking to sales. Its speech detail page includes specifications and a sample action. One inspected page showed different hour totals in prose and its specification table; that is a reason to keep Avlys counts in one versioned source, not a conclusion about the competitor’s underlying data. [Catalog](https://defined.ai/datasets), [speech detail](https://defined.ai/datasets/spanish-call-center).

### C. Describe the process, not just the adjective “quality”

iMerit explains collection protocols, task categories, and review stages on its robotics page. That is more informative positioning than an unsupported blanket accuracy claim. [Collection page](https://imerit.ai/domains/robotics/video-data-collection/).

Avlys should publish an anonymized example batch report: number reviewed, review method, defect categories, acceptance threshold, and rework. Establish thresholds with the buyer; no universal percentage suits every task.

### D. Egocentric video is a distinct product

Defined.ai’s household collection describes video alongside IMU measurements; Scale describes both teleoperation and robotless capture. These are materially different packages. [Household dataset](https://defined.ai/datasets/household-robotics), [Scale Physical AI](https://scale.com/physical-ai).

Human first-person recordings can be useful for robot learning, but human and robot bodies/actions differ. Research such as HumanEgo addresses this transfer explicitly; footage should not automatically be described as robot control trajectories. Buyer usefulness depends on the intended pipeline and representations. [HumanEgo research abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24934).

For Avlys, disclose the camera setup, task, labels, and any sensors actually present. Distinguish measured depth or pose from model-estimated values. Do not promise force measurements, calibrated action data, or robot compatibility that the samples cannot demonstrate.

## 3. Proposed positioning

Proposed navigation label: **AI Training Data**.

Proposed page identity: **Avlys Data**.

Proposed headline:

> Text, image, and voice data for AI training and evaluation.

Supporting copy, conditional on validating the advertised services:

> Explore sample collections or discuss a custom collection brief. Review the formats, annotations, and delivery requirements before starting a pilot.

Secondary egocentric line:

> Egocentric capture pilots for robotics and embodied AI.

Primary action: **Explore samples**. Secondary action: **Discuss a data requirement**. A dataset detail page should offer **Request an evaluation sample**.

Avoid vague “powering the future” claims, unverified customer logos, giant inventory numbers, compliance badges without evidence, and claims that all data is ready for every model. Do not claim that being multilingual or India-based is unique; the reviewed providers already market broad language and collection coverage.

The potential advantage to test is a clearly documented, relevant collection with responsive delivery. This is not yet a demonstrated competitive advantage. Choose one or two initial domains after inspecting your samples instead of claiming to serve all industries equally well.

## 4. Offers and first buyer hypotheses

| Proposed offer | What the buyer receives | Launch condition |
|---|---|---|
| License an existing collection | Approved files, manifest, schema, usage terms, quality notes | Commercial rights and inventory verified |
| Commission collection | An agreed capture protocol, pilot batch, review report, then accepted production batches | People, equipment, permissions, cost, and capacity validated |
| Annotation and QA | Buyer-authorized data labeled to an agreed schema, with review and corrections | Tooling and reviewer capability demonstrated |

Do not sell expert-generated training answers or specialist evaluation solely because text files exist. Confirm the relevant expertise and review standards first.

Suggested initial customer hypotheses:

- Speech and voice-agent teams: useful languages, accents, environments, transcripts, and speaker metadata, where available.
- Vision and document-processing teams: task-relevant images, document/extraction pairs, and consistent labels.
- Language-model teams: a specific domain or task collection with traceable sources and useful supervision, not simply a pile of prose.
- Robotics teams: a narrow capture pilot matched to a requested task and setup.

These are prioritization hypotheses, not validated demand. Interview buyers about a real sample and request a paid pilot before investing in large-scale speculative capture.

### A second route: supply existing data companies

Defined.ai publicly distinguishes dataset-owner partnerships from collection/annotation service suppliers. It describes checks on quality, metadata, rights, and compliance. This is a concrete channel worth investigating after preparing your evidence pack; it does not establish Avlys eligibility, acceptance, or expected revenue. [Partner program](https://defined.ai/partnership-programs).

Recommend two sales paths: direct model/application teams and qualified data-platform partnerships. No outreach or application has been made.

### Commercial structure to test

Use an evaluation sample, followed by a paid pilot, explicit acceptance, and then larger batches. Quote using defined accepted units and scope: records/pages, images, audio hours, or video episodes/hours; add annotation complexity, setup, and license restrictions where appropriate.

Measure actual collection time, reviewer time, rejection/rework, storage, and acquisition cost before setting rates. Do not borrow unsupported market prices or guarantee delivery capacity from sample counts.

## 5. Proposed page and portfolio

Keep the enterprise software homepage intact. Add a distinct `/data` destination. An existing `/services/ai-training-data` route should later be assessed for cross-linking or intentional consolidation so two pages do not repeat the same proposition.

Recommended page sequence:

1. Precise headline, two actions, and an authentic cleared sample preview.
2. Sample gallery with Text/Documents, Images, and Voice/Audio filters; Egocentric marked as a pilot category.
3. Three ways to work together: existing collections, custom collection, annotation/QA.
4. How collection, review, and delivery work, using actual evidence.
5. A smaller egocentric pilot section with a real task clip.
6. Sample access, provenance, use restrictions, and delivery FAQ.
7. A short requirements form.

Start with a small curated gallery rather than hundreds of thin entries. An editorial target of roughly 8–12 collections is optional; publish fewer if only a few pass review. This is not an estimate of your current inventory.

### What visitors should see

| Modality | Useful preview | Details beside it |
|---|---|---|
| Text/documents | Cleared excerpt or document crop alongside structured labels | Language, domain, task, schema, source type, record/page count |
| Images | Real source image with an optional annotation overlay | Categories, annotation type, resolution, format, review method |
| Voice/audio | Short approved clip with transcript; no autoplay | Locale, recording conditions, speaker labeling, timestamps, codec/sample rate |
| Egocentric | Short task episode with an annotation timeline | Task, camera setup, frame rate, sensors, episode boundaries, label provenance |

Generated illustrations may explain a process if clearly labeled, but should never serve as evidence of actual captured data. A simple real sample plus a legible schema is preferable to decorative AI imagery.

### Dataset card requirements

Use a consistent card and detailed record for every collection. Dataset documentation commonly includes contents, intended use, limitations, and licensing metadata; Hugging Face provides a useful reference. [Dataset-card documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/datasets-cards).

Recommended Avlys fields:

- Collection ID, version, update date, modality, task, and availability status.
- Actual accepted volume with units; show preview size separately from total inventory.
- Languages, locales, domain, collection period, environments, and relevant capture devices.
- Format, schema, sample files, label taxonomy, and annotation method, including model assistance.
- Review process, checked count, defect definitions, acceptance criteria, and known limitations.
- Split and deduplication method; account for speaker, session, location, or document-family leakage where relevant.
- Source/provenance, commercial-use permissions, public-preview approval, and restrictions.
- Delivery package and evaluation-sample request action.

Keep counts and availability in a single maintained record so cards, downloads, and sales proposals agree.

## 6. Samples, rights, and storage before publishing

Your clarification confirmed the modalities, not permission to publish or license them. Treat public preview rights and commercial training rights as separate review questions. Defined.ai’s partner review and license materials illustrate that rights and agreed uses are part of the product, not merely a website checkbox. They are not a contract template for Avlys. [Partner requirements](https://defined.ai/partnership-programs), [license terms](https://defined.ai/data-license-agreement).

Recommended release gates:

1. Record who collected or supplied each collection and the supporting agreements/permissions.
2. Review personal information, confidential client content, identifiable faces/voices, third-party works, and restrictions before selecting previews.
3. Confirm permitted public display, evaluation, training, onward supply, retention, and any exclusions with qualified legal review where needed.
4. Approve a sanitized preview and document what it represents; do not claim redaction alone resolves every rights issue.

Suggested access levels for the later build:

- **Public:** a small, approved preview and dataset card.
- **Evaluation:** a controlled sample pack under agreed terms.
- **Delivery:** private customer access to authorized files.

Do not put proprietary datasets in Git or the website’s public asset directory. A later implementation can use private object storage, internal upload/review controls, and time-limited access. No storage platform has been selected or configured. The initial page does not need a public upload portal or payment marketplace.

## 7. Decisions before building

First prepare a collection inventory: modality, domain, language, approximate amount, annotations, source, permissions, and one candidate preview per collection. Then inspect representative files and measure quality instead of assuming the entire collection matches a showcase item.

The first two questions to resolve are:

1. Which languages and subject areas make up your strongest text, image, and voice collections?
2. Were they collected by your team or obtained from others, and what permission exists to publicly show and commercially license them?

After that, choose the initial offers, approve accurate dataset cards, and agree the page brief. Only then start the website implementation. If “saving data” means an internal upload library, scope that separately from the public portfolio.
