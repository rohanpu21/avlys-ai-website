## Design Context

### Users
SMB founders evaluating AI automation partners. They visit to quickly judge credibility, scope of services, and the fastest path to a strategy call.

### Brand Personality
Fast, AI-ready, gets work done. Confident, technical, and execution-focused.

### Aesthetic Direction
xAI-inspired dark-first brutalist minimalism: near-black background, white typography, monospace display, sharp edges, no gradients or shadows. Emphasis on whitespace and high-contrast reading.

### Design Principles
- Speed to clarity: highlight value proposition and CTA within the first viewport.
- Technical trust: restrained palette, precise typography, and minimal ornamentation.
- Modular proof: clear, scannable solution blocks and portfolio cards.
- Conversion focus: consistent, prominent strategy call actions.
- Responsive by design: preserve hierarchy and whitespace on mobile.
